# Cantrip

Cantrip is a tool for running tabletop role-playing games: the Game Master prepares and drives the table, the Players follow what the Game Master shows them.

## Language

### Content

**Game**:
A body of play prepared by the Game Master, holding a title and its Scenes.
_Avoid_: Session, Campaign, Adventure

**Scene**:
One moment of a Game that the Game Master can show to the Players, identified to people by its title. It belongs to exactly one Game for its whole life.
_Avoid_: Page, Slide, Encounter

**Active Scene**:
The Scene of a Game that the Players currently see. A Game has at most one; it may have none.
_Avoid_: Current scene, Live scene, Shown scene

### Modes

**Game Master mode**:
The way of using Cantrip that manages Games and Scenes and chooses each Game's Active Scene. It is a mode of the app, not a user account: there is a single, implicit Game Master.
_Avoid_: Admin, GM account, Owner

**Player mode**:
The way of using Cantrip that follows one Game and shows only its Active Scene, without any management.
_Avoid_: Viewer, Spectator, Player account
